New tools for everyday developer tasks, with smoother handling of large inputs.

### What's New

- **CSV Converter** — convert CSV files to JSON or JSON back to CSV directly in your browser.

- **UUID Generator** — create UUIDs quickly whenever you need unique identifiers.

- **Text Diff** — compare two pieces of text with inline word-level highlighting, so changes are easy to spot.

- **Timestamp Converter** — convert timestamps and dates into the format you need.

- **User-Agent Parser** — inspect a browser or device user-agent string and share the result with a link.

- **Regex Tester** — test regular expressions with live match highlighting.

### Improvements

- **Shareable tool links** — Base64, hash, JWT, color, JSON, and user-agent tools can now preserve their settings in a link, making it easier to share or return to your work.

- **Smoother large-input tools** — text comparison and regular-expression matching now stay responsive for longer or more complex inputs.

### Bug Fixes

- **Safer contact QR codes** — vCard fields are now handled safely when generating QR codes.

- **More reliable shared links** — tool settings survive navigation and round trips more consistently.
