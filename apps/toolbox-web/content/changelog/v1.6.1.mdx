The EV Charging Estimator now uses real-world charger data for more accurate results, with gentler behavior when you're editing inputs.

### Improvements

- **Real-world efficiency data** — efficiency rates now reflect actual charger performance (7kW at 86%, 25kW at 87%, 70kW at 83%, 85kW at 85%) instead of a flat estimate
- **Graduated SOC penalty** — battery charging slowdown now scales with state of charge: mild at 80–90%, moderate at 90–95%, and steep at 95–100%
- **Smarter charger defaults** — selecting a charger type (AC Level 1, AC Level 2, DC Fast, DC Ultra) now pre-fills the charging power with its real-world default
- **Less intrusive editing** — advanced fields stay empty while you type and only fill with defaults on blur, so the form doesn't fight your input

### Bug Fixes

- **Validation timing** — end-of-charge validation now clears immediately when you fix the start SOC, instead of staying stuck
- **Field reset behavior** — advanced fields now reset on blur instead of on every keystroke, preventing accidental overwrites while typing
