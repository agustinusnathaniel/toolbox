A new tool for electric vehicle owners. Estimate how much energy you need to charge your EV between two battery levels, accounting for charging losses across AC and DC charging types.

### What's Included

- Choose from Level 1 AC, Level 2 AC, DC Fast, and DC Ultra charging
- See estimated kWh required, charging time, and cost
- Input fields for battery capacity, usable energy, and your electricity rate
- Your inputs are saved automatically between visits
